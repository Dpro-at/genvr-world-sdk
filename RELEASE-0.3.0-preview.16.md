# 0.3.0-preview.16

The motion lab grows into a full workshop, and every body plays motions more truly.

Motion lab: made motions by pose and key frames (joint picker, sliders, mouse tools, key track); every move checked on the Lab body in three motion styles (foot slide, ground, joint limits, body clash, balance, loop seam, speed match) with each style's limits as numbers the owner sets; eleven training stations in the yard (jump pit, hurdles, high bar, grasp table, conveyor, tool bench, pool, climbing wall, ladder, weapons rack with foam and wood training props, target post), each with its own scores, and Run all stations; pick who runs (the Avatar tab's character, the robot or My characters). Private motions a person imported in Avatar › My motions can be tried, checked, run and compared side by side or as a see-through ghost over one of our moves; a saved lab project keeps only a short key, never the file or its name.

Moves: no move puts a foot under the floor on any body, planted feet stay locked, turns lift the stepping foot and crouches keep the weight over the feet. Made motions play in the game (My motions, keys 1–8, base moves) and travel with a world package.

Imported characters and motions: an imported motion keeps its scale (rigs in centimetres), its limb twist and a level pelvis on any body, and comes down to the floor when it never touched it; imported characters keep their face shapes, so expressions and the speaking mouth work; realistic bodies keep their proportions when Shape changes the height. Characters and motions can be deleted from the library, and files or folders can be dropped on My characters and My motions.

Our own motion files: a motion can be played on our skeleton and written as our own animation keys (`bakeMotion`); a host can register a private motion library of such files, which plays through the same clip pipeline as the Core moves. The local Studio server can serve an owner's private library from a folder outside the package (local address only).

Bodies: the Standard robot (five toes, round screen eyes) is the default and the only body offered in this release; earlier bodies still open for old worlds. Bodies marked local (models kept only on the owner's device) are offered only when the host finds their model, and releases leave their files out.

No method scans or uploads private libraries, voices or model weights. Local storage keys, project and library IDs and voice assignments are unchanged; older projects and layouts open as before.
